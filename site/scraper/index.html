FrogScrapper - easy game boxart in 3 steps

Choose a ROM folder or SD card, find matching artwork, and download a ZIP laid out for your frontend. Your ROM files stay on your device.

After the scrape

Put the artwork on your card

  1. Download the ZIP.When the scrape finishes, save the artwork ZIP to your computer.
  2. Extract it beside your ROM folders. Use the same folder level you selected below: the SD card root, the Roms folder, or the system folder. Keep the folders from the ZIP so each image lands beside the right games. Extract it at the SD card root. For muOS, extract the ZIP at the root of the SD card, even if you selected the Roms folder or a system folder. The ZIP contains the MUOS/info/catalogue/... paths.
  3. Eject the card safely and check your games.Put the card back in your handheld and open the game list. The selected frontend layout determines where the artwork is installed.
