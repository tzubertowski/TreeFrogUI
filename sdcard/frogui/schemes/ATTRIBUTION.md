## License review status

I inspected ten current `.muxthm` archives from the MustardOS theme release; each contained `credits.txt` but no license, licence, copying, copyright, or readme file declaring terms. The locally extracted scheme folders likewise contain credits but no per-theme license notice. The CatOS attribution was verified from the local extracted folder and the catalogue; its separate current archive was not downloaded for this check.

The MustardOS wiki pages are marked CC BY-NC 4.0, but that notice applies to wiki content and is not a blanket license for community theme assets. The [MustardOS Terms of Use](https://muos.dev/docs/tou) say catalogue availability does not grant a license to third-party packages and that each item's own terms continue to apply. The MustardOS theme repository is marked GPL-3.0, but no individual license accompanied the downloaded theme archives, so this project does not assume that repository license clears every community theme or its embedded artwork.

Credits are not permission. Redistribution rights for the imported theme designs and artwork remain unverified. This demo includes them at the maintainer's direction with their credits retained; do not carry them into a stable release until the applicable licenses or author permissions are confirmed. Keep this notice and the upstream credits with any cleared theme ports.
